# Logarithmic

Logarithmic is a simple app for journaling, planning, and note-taking. It consists of entries in a logbook that have a parent/child relationship to each other. Entries are thus organized into trees, and are placed in columns that indicate their level of detail (with the "roots" of the trees having higher column numbers and the "leaves" having lower column numbers.) Each entry has a name; each entry can have content and metadata (but might not.)

It is inspired by Linear's approach to initiatives, projects, and issues; however, it has a more generalized, less opinionated data model.
